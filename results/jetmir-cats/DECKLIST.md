## By package

### body count (21)
- **Adorned Pouncer** {1}{W} · Creature — Cat
- **Feline Sovereign** {2}{G} · Creature — Cat  — Its combat-damage trigger destroys an artifact or enchantment, which is the only repeatable answer in the deck to the taxing permanents that turn off attacking.
- **King of the Pride** {2}{W} · Creature — Cat
- **Ocelot Pride** {W} · Creature — Cat  — Lifelink on the lords turns the end-step trigger on, and the city's blessing turns it into a doubler for each token that entered that turn.
- **Pride Sovereign** {2}{G} · Creature — Cat
- **Prideful Parent** {2}{W} · Creature — Cat
- **Regal Caracal** {3}{W}{W} · Creature — Cat  — Three bodies on arrival, and the lifelink it hands out is what switches on the end-step trigger elsewhere in the deck.
- **Sacred Cat** {W} · Creature — Cat
- **Ajani's Chosen** {2}{W}{W} · Creature — Cat Soldier
- **Leonin Warleader** {2}{W}{W} · Creature — Cat Soldier
- **Nacatl War-Pride** {3}{G}{G}{G} · Creature — Cat Warrior  — On attack it makes one tapped, attacking copy of itself for each creature the defender controls, and each copy inherits the must-be-blocked-by-exactly-one clause. It is the one card that takes the board from six to nine inside a combat step rather than across two turns.
- **Wild Nacatl** {G} · Creature — Cat Warrior
- **Qasali Pridemage** {G}{W} · Creature — Cat Wizard
- **Arahbo, the First Fang** {2}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Avatar  — Each nontoken Cat cast afterwards arrives with a second body attached, which converts one card into two toward the thresholds.
- **Kaheera, the Orphanguard** {1}{G/W}{G/W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Beast  — The restriction makes the companion condition true by construction, since a deck whose creature cards are Cats satisfies it. Kaheera is played here inside the 99; leaving this slot for another Cat and running her from outside the game is a real option and is set out in the pilot notes.
- **Kemba, Kha Enduring** {1}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Cleric
- **Brimaz, King of Oreskos** {1}{W}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Soldier
- **Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar** {1}{G}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Warrior  — The printed text in the file stops opponents casting spells during my turn, which is the answer to the Fog problem that the pool otherwise lacks. Its draw trigger fires whenever creatures with power above base power connect, and the commander's anthem makes that condition true for the whole board.
- **Marisi, Breaker of the Coil** {1}{R}{G}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Warrior  — Opponents cannot cast spells during combat, which protects the alpha strike from instant-speed answers. Goading their board afterwards is what keeps the third opponent occupied on the turn the deck cannot kill them.
- **Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist** {1}{G}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Warrior
- **Ajani, Nacatl Pariah // Ajani, Nacatl Avenger** {1}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Warrior // Legendary Planeswalker — Ajani  · as Ajani, Nacatl Pariah

### damage conversion (7)
- **Lightning Greaves** {2} · Artifact — Equipment
- **Impact Tremors** {1}{R} · Enchantment  — One damage per body entering is small per trigger and large across a doubled token turn; it also kills through a Fog, which combat damage does not.
- **Great Train Heist** {R} · Instant
- **Return of the Wildspeaker** {4}{G} · Instant
- **Jazal Goldmane** {2}{W}{W} · Legendary Creature — Cat Warrior  — The activated ability gives attacking creatures +X/+X where X is the number of attackers, so it scales with the same count the commander's tiers scale with. At eight attackers it is +8/+8 spread across the board.
- **Biorhythm** {6}{G}{G} · Sorcery  — Each player's life total becomes their creature count, which is a one-sided ender at nine Cats against opponents holding one or two bodies. Eight mana is the price of the only non-combat kill in the pool.
- **Makindi Stampede // Makindi Mesas** {3}{W}{W} · Sorcery // Land  · as Makindi Stampede  — Taken as the sorcery. The land face is why it beat a strictly larger pump spell: a flooded hand can play it as a white source instead.

### flex (2)
- **Smothering Tithe** {3}{W} · Enchantment  — The Treasures pay for the commander's tax spiral and for a Jazal activation in the same turn as a threshold-crossing token burst.
- **Second Harvest** {2}{G}{G} · Instant  — At instant speed it doubles the token half of the board, which is how a board of five becomes a board of nine before blockers are declared.

### token engines (6)
- **Anointed Procession** {3}{W} · Enchantment  — It creates no token on its own; it doubles what the rest of the package creates, which is why it sits here rather than in a support slot.
- **Felidar Retreat** {3}{W} · Enchantment
- **Mirror March** {5}{R} · Enchantment  — The weakest slot in the deck: six mana, coin flips, and the tokens leave at the next end step. It is here because the pool holds four noncreature permanents that make Cat tokens and this package asked for six.
- **Renewed Solidarity** {2}{W} · Enchantment  — Naming Cat gives the team +1/+0 and copies each Cat token that entered that turn at end step, so it compounds with the doubler rather than repeating it.
- **Caretaker's Talent** {2}{W} · Enchantment — Class
- **Esika's Chariot** {3}{G} · Legendary Artifact — Vehicle  — Two bodies on arrival and a copy of a token on each attack. Crew 4 is easy once the anthem tiers are running.
